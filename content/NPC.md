# NPC

**Entity Type:** Non-Player Character

## Overview

NPCs represent all other intelligent beings the player can interact with in the Sleeping Empire universe. They range from powerful faction leaders and ancient subordinates to desperate traders and hostile raiders.

## Components

- **Position**
- **Stats**
- **Inventory**
- **Equipment**
- **Faction Affiliation**
- **Dialogue / Behavior Tree**

## Types of NPCs

- **Merchants** — Offer trade and rare items
- **Faction Agents** — Represent political powers
- **Ancient Subordinates** — Extremely powerful beings from the Empire's past
- **Hostile Entities** — Bandits, corrupted beings, or Nullborn-touched
- **Wanderers** — Other ambitious individuals like the player

## Interaction

NPCs can provide:
- Quests and opportunities
- Trade
- Information (Scriptures)
- Combat challenges
